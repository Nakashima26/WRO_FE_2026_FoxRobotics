# FoxRobotics replay viewer

The track is the main view. The camera and bird’s-eye view share a column so their frames can be compared at the same timestamp. File-based frames preserve the original twin’s track palette, obstacle colors and vehicle silhouette.

Use the dark MATLAB viewer background RGB (0.08, 0.10, 0.13) and near-white labels RGB (0.95, 0.96, 0.98). Keep the title at 18 pt and give each image a functional heading. Use native MATLAB controls for focus and keyboard access. Keep playback paused on open, with explicit play/pause, adjacent-frame buttons and one shared timeline. Always display elapsed and total simulation time.
